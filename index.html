Helpful to know

Frequently asked questions

Does LEXORA provide legal advice?

This prototype provides general legal information only. Individual legal advice requires review by an appropriately qualified advocate, subject to applicable professional rules.

Can I ask about property matters in another Indian state?

You can use the topic areas to identify general questions. Property laws, land records and procedures may vary by state and the facts of the matter.

Can I upload my property documents?

Document upload is not enabled in this prototype. A secure upload facility, access controls, retention policy and privacy safeguards must be implemented before accepting documents.

Does submitting an enquiry make LEXORA my lawyer?

No. A website enquiry alone does not create an advocate-client relationship. Any engagement must be separately and properly agreed.
